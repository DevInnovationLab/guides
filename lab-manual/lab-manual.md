## Things everyone is expected to be doing

Four recurring obligations that are easy to miss because nobody chases you for them.

* **Slack, not email.** If everyone on a message is Lab personnel other than Michael, it belongs in Slack. Email is for Michael and external collaborators.
* **Asana for work, not memory.** Anything you agreed to do in a meeting becomes a task.
* **The travel and leave calendar.** Time off and trips go on the shared calendar, in addition to any formal request.
* **The monthly time tracking form.** First Friday of every month.

<hr class="section-divider">

## How the manual is organized

Four parts, roughly in order of how soon you will need them.

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **General Information** | The bulk of the manual. Onboarding, office and equipment, IRB, lab logistics and management expectations, communication and office norms, memos, data, projects, offboarding |
| **Ops & Admin** | Professional development, reimbursement, relocation, and visa fee policies |
| **Specialized Topics** | Thirteen how-to modules for people already onboarded: templates, Asana, travel, contracts, data sharing, SurveyCTO, Box, Drive, grants, computing, RAs, literature reviews |
| **FAQs, Kenya FAQ, India FAQ** | Questions that come up repeatedly, including safety, holidays, complaints, and where to get support |

Two navigation quirks worth knowing. The **Specialized Topics** items lost their heading formatting, so they do not appear in the Google Docs outline pane even though they are listed in the table of contents. And **Ops & Admin** is missing from the table of contents entirely. If you cannot find something in the outline, search the doc text.

## When the manual is not the right place

* **Analysis, code, and reproducibility.** See the [DIL Analytics Manual](https://devinnovationlab.github.io/guides/){: .btn .btn-more }{:target="_blank"}
* **What data may be stored where.** See the [UChicago Data Usage Guide](https://dataguide.uchicago.edu/){: .btn .btn-more }{:target="_blank"} and DIL's own data guide.
* **Anything project-specific.** Ask the lead researcher, or check the project's Slack channel.
* **Anything the manual gets wrong or leaves out.** Do not edit the doc directly. Add a task to the [manual suggestions Asana project](https://app.asana.com/0/1202382439110325/1202382439110325){: .btn .btn-internal }{:target="_blank"}
